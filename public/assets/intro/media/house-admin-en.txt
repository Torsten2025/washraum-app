WaschZeit for house administrators

1. Separate your plan and administration [00:00:00.000]
My Plan shows your own appointments if you are also a resident. Manage is where you work for your building. Your actions remain personally accountable.

2. Prioritize the administration overview [00:00:08.600]
Start with open tasks in the overview. Check incidents and blocks first. The links take you to the relevant area.

3. Apartments and access cards [00:00:16.600]
Enter the apartment and doorbell name. Email remains optional. Create a personal access card. It works once, within seven days. Hand it to the right person.

4. Accounts and recovery [00:00:28.320]
Create an additional QR card for another person in the same apartment. A recovery code is only for eligible residents without verified email. Check their identity first.

5. Manage equipment and rooms [00:00:39.080]
Building and Equipment shows the resources in your building. Clear names help people book. Blocking a resource requires an explicit decision.

6. Machine and room logbook [00:00:48.240]
The logbook separates new, ongoing and completed cases. Open a case and check the resource and history. Personal reports are kept separately.

7. Handle an incident through release [00:00:58.360]
After taking a case, record the repair. Only a successful function check closes it and releases an incident-related block.

8. Maintain recurring appointments [00:01:06.720]
Recurring appointments are for justified regular use. Choose the equipment, time and recurrence. Check the preview before saving the series.

9. Understand analytics [00:01:16.000]
Analytics shows usage and available capacity in the building. Use it to manage operations, not to rank residents.

10. Confirm critical actions [00:01:23.640]
Critical actions show the target and impact before confirmation. Cancel if you are unsure. Never share your own password.

11. Know the house administrator boundaries [00:01:31.720]
Your administration is limited to your own building. Other buildings and global system settings are not house administrator tasks.
