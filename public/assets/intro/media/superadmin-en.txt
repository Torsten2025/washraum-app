WaschZeit for super administrators

1. Keep multiple houses in view [00:00:00.000]
The overview brings together tasks and warnings. Check which building each item belongs to before taking action.

2. Switch the active house [00:00:07.360]
Choose the active building at the top. Administration reloads its data. Check the building name before making changes.

3. Manage houses [00:00:15.120]
Building and Equipment separates locations from equipment. Deactivating a building is not routine cleanup. Check the consequences first.

4. Manage roles safely [00:00:24.200]
Apartment membership and administration permissions are different things. An additional QR card creates a resident sign-in, not administrator rights.

5. Super administrator rights and handover [00:00:33.480]
A trusted deputy protects against losing administrative access. Check the person and permissions carefully. Handover does not mean sharing a password.

6. Backups and external storage [00:00:43.000]
A backup needs a tested recovery path. Use only approved download and protected storage. External backup integrations are blocked in production.

7. Control maintenance mode [00:00:52.640]
Maintenance is planned operational work, not an everyday switch. It requires backup, authorization and checks afterwards. This recording does not start maintenance.

8. Respect the production restriction [00:01:03.800]
The global pilot reset is blocked in production. Do not use it for cleanup. Data removal needs a separately reviewed task.

9. Review audit and system status [00:01:12.000]
System shows service and operational status. The audit records administrative actions. Do not silently remove technical records.

10. Perform critical actions safely [00:01:21.200]
Before a major action, check the building, target and recovery path. Finish deliberately. Sign out on shared devices.
