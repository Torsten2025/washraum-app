Welcome to WaschZeit

1. Sign in and set up your profile [00:00:00.000]
Your personal access card works without email. Choose your password and keep your sign-in ID safe.

2. Explore the week and month [00:00:06.480]
Switch between week and month to find available times. Choose an upcoming day. Occupied times stay protected.

3. Book a laundry package [00:00:14.040]
Choose an available time and your washing machine. Add drying if needed. Review the summary and book. Your completed package appears in My Bookings.

4. Manage your bookings [00:00:25.560]
My Bookings shows the appointments for your apartment. You can cancel a future booking. The time then becomes available again.

5. Release early or cancel [00:00:33.560]
Finished washing early? Choose Release early on your current booking. Someone else can then use the machine.

6. Optional email and device push [00:00:40.440]
Email is optional. You can save your settings without an address. Choose notification filters. Set up push separately on each device in App and Device.

7. Report a problem [00:00:51.000]
Report a problem with the affected machine or room. Describe only what you observed. Status notifications are optional.

8. Connect another household member [00:00:59.000]
Open the household QR invitation in your profile. The code works once, for ten minutes. No account yet? Leave the ID empty and choose your own password. Already registered? Sign in with your resident ID and password.

9. Calendar subscription and help [00:01:14.960]
In App and Device, subscribe to your apartment calendar. Keep the address private. Help contains the rules and introduction. Sign out on shared devices.
